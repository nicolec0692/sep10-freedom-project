# Entry 2
##### 11/16/26

## Context

Since my last blog entry, l have been reaserching how technology is changing the way people cook. I found different websites, applications, and kitchen appliances that help people prepare food more easily, learn new recipes, and keep track of cooking. I started by looking for cooking applications that are beginner-friendly, and then I expanded my research to include more advanced software and hardware. l discovered that technology can help in many different ways, from finding recipes and planning meals to automatically controlling cooking. This research helped me understand how cooking has changed over time and how inventions can make cooking more convenient
 
## What l found and How

While researching, l found several cooking applications, including [Kitchen Stories](https://www.kitchenstories.com), [SideChef](https://www.sidechef.com), [Samsung Food}(https://samsungfood.com), and [Crouton}(https://crouton.app). Kitchen stories provides thousands of recipes, create grocery lists, and plan meals. SideChef also provides guided recipes and step-by-step instructions, pictures and videos. I also explored more advanced cooking software, such as [Crouton](https://crouton.app) which organizes recipes and can use artificial intelligence to recognize recipes from pictures. It can create grocery lists, organize meal plans, and automatically create cooking timers from recipes. I also researched [Samsung Food](https://www.samsungfood.com/) which allows people to search for recipes based on ingredients they already have at home and provides information about different cooking methods. After researching software, I began looking into hardware, which includes physical cooking devices that use technology. I discovered smart ovens, such as the [KitchenAid](https://www.kitchenaid.com/major-appliances/wall-ovens/oven-cooking-camera.html) and the [Tovala Smart Oven](https://www.tovala.com/smart-ovens) These appliances use cameras, sensors, and different cooking settings to help prepare food. The KitchenAid oven can recognize certain foods and allow users to select how they want their food cooked. The Tovala Smart Oven combines different cooking functions, including baking, toasting, and air frying, and can use scanners to read supported grocery products.
