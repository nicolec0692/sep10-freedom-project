## What l found and How

While researching, l found several cooking applications, including [Kitchen Stories](https://www.kitchenstories.com), [SideChef](https://www.sidechef.com), [Samsung Food](https://samsungfood.com), and [Crouton}(https://crouton.app). Kitchen stories provides thousands of recipes, create grocery lists, and plan meals. SideChef also provides guided recipes and step-by-step instructions, pictures and videos. I also explored more advanced cooking software, such as [Crouton](https://crouton.app) which organizes recipes and can use artificial intelligence to recognize recipes from pictures. It can create grocery lists, organize meal plans, and automatically create cooking timers from recipes. I also researched [Samsung Food](https://www.samsungfood.com/) which allows people to search for recipes based on ingredients they already have at home and provides information about different cooking methods.
